Architecture & Mathematical Normalization Engine
ISL Mathematical Formulation

Deterministic Screen-x Sorting

MediaPipe landmarkers frequently flip hand identities (Left vs Right) during two-handed interactions. We sort hands deterministically based on screen-x wrist position ($h_1.x \le h_2.x$), eliminating classification flipping completely.

Relative Coordinate Normalization

Hand 1 is anchored to its wrist ($P_1 - W_1 = 0$). Hand 2 is anchored relative to Hand 1's wrist ($P_2 - W_1$), preserving the critical physical distance vector between both interacting hands across 126 coordinate features.

Deep Neural Network Topology

Input(126) $\to$ Dense(256) + BatchNorm + Dropout(0.2) $\to$ Dense(256) + BatchNorm + Dropout(0.2) $\to$ Dense(128) $\to$ Dense(64) $\to$ Dense(26, Softmax). Total 177,754 parameters trained for 50 epochs.
